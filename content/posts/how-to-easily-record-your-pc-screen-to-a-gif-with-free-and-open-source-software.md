A couple of folks have asked how I quickly post a snippet of my recorded screen in Slack or how I created the gifs in my previous posts. The software I use is free and open source, so I'm happy to share [ScreenToGif](https://www.screentogif.com) and give the dev, [@NickeManarin](https://twitter.com) some love.

What's even cooler, is Nicke Manarin's website for ScreenToGif itself is open-source. You can find [it's repo here.](https://github.com)
