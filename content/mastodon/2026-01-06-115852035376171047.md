There are methodologies for what one ought to believe - Universities, research institutes, and agencies like the Max Planck Society discover new knowledge; libraries, museums, and archives such as the Library of Congress and archive.org preserve it; publishers, educators, and journalists from places like Nature, public schools, and newsrooms organize and validate it; and communities and cultures contribute essential lived and traditional knowledge that formal institutions often miss.
